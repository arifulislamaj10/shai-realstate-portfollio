HOW TO ADD SHAN SHAI'S PROFILE PHOTO
=====================================

Facebook blocks automatic photo download, so please add her photo manually:

1. Open: https://www.facebook.com/shai.dane
2. Click on her profile picture to view it full size
3. Right-click the photo → "Save image as..."
4. Save the file as: shan-shai.jpg
5. Put it in this folder: shan-shai-website\images\

The website will automatically show her photo in the Hero and About sections.

Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png (rename to shan-shai.jpg)
